By using this website or the phone app you accept the following. They are short because the project is small.

What this is

A prototype built in 24 hours at OxHack 2026. It estimates the wait at the hackathon food point and forecasts it 15 to 60 minutes ahead. It is free to use and is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind.

Estimates can be wrong

  • The current wait is computed from sensor counts. Sensors miss people, so the number can be off by a few minutes.
  • The forecast comes from a model trained on Disney World ride queues. On this queue its accuracy has not been checked yet. Treat its range as a guide, not a promise.
  • A screen labelled Replay shows a recorded rush, not the queue right now.

Do not rely on QueueTime for anything that matters more than dinner. If you have a dietary or medical need, talk to the organisers instead of waiting for a recommendation.

Reminders

A reminder only goes off while the page is open on your device, and only if the laptop running QueueTime is reachable. If it does not go off, nobody is liable for the pizza you missed.

Fair use

Do not try to break the server, flood it with requests, or interfere with the sensors. They are taped to a table and easily upset.
